About The Fest

What is AVLOKAN?

A platform where engineering students, faculty, and industry professionals collaborate, compete, and innovate over two electrifying days.

AVLOKAN is the annual technical festival of the Association of Civil Engineers (ACE), Department of Civil Engineering, Birsa Institute of Technology (BIT) Sindri — organised to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world civil engineering challenges.

Conducted over two days, AVLOKAN brings together engineering students from across the country to apply classroom theory to real-world problems — with emphasis on civil infrastructure, sustainable development, and digital engineering.

The festival features Conclave, Technical Paper Presentations, Neuro Nexus, Game of Truss, Survey Treasure Hunt, Model Exhibitions, and concludes with cultural performances and a grand valedictory ceremony.

2024

AVLOKAN '24

AVLOKAN '24 was the second edition of the annual technical fest organized by the Department of Civil Engineering under the aegis of ACE. The multi-day event was designed to revive students' love for civil engineering through a series of engaging and competitive activities. It provided a platform for students to apply theoretical knowledge to practical challenges, fostering discovery, observation, and innovation.
